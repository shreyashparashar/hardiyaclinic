Frequently Asked Questions

All questions
What is the working mechanism of braces?

Braces fix the teeth in the right place, at the right angle and with proper pressure. It has been developed through many years of study and testing. And it is authentic. When braces are applied, they apply pressure on the teeth and jaws according to their shape. So that the teeth and facial bones can develop in the right direction.

What Precautions should be taken during treatment?

During the treatment, the advice and rules given by the orthodontist doctor to the patient must be followed. It is important to take care of the braces fitted by the doctor. Consumption of hard food items like grains, chewing gum, chocolate, chips, and fast food etc. can hinder the treatment. The design of braces is tailored to the individual's teeth, which must be maintained properly.

What is the appropriate time for orthodontic treatment?

A first orthodontic check-up around the age of 7 to 8 helps spot jaw and bite problems early. Braces are most often fitted between 10 and 14 years of age, while the jaws are still growing, but teeth can be moved at any age, so adults can be treated too.
