Hardiya Dental Clinic & Orthodontic Centre. Expert. Caring. Affordable.

The expert care you deserve

Hardiya Dental Clinic & Orthodontic Centre is based in Geeta Bhavan, Indore and is dedicated to providing expert orthodontic care. Dr. Sumeet Hardiya is an experienced orthodontist who handles patients with great care. Our clinic offers a range of services, including braces and Invisalign. We believe in providing affordable care without compromising on quality. Our team is committed to making your experience with us comfortable and stress-free. This is the place to come if you are looking for a perfect smile. Dr. Hardiya and his team have helped numerous patients achieve their dream smile. We are passionate about what we do and are dedicated to providing the best possible care to our patients.
